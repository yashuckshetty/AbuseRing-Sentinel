Core Architecture Thesis

"Abuse risk is an evolving process involving multiple sources of evidence. When behavioral and structural evidence agree, automation may be appropriate. When they disagree, disagreement itself becomes a risk signal that changes the routing decision and sends the case toward human investigation rather than hiding uncertainty inside a single score."

CRITICAL SUBSET FINDING: Referral Farming (Unseen Ring Topology)

Under threshold ablation, 100% of evaluable referral-farming accounts (107/107) are silently missed as False Negatives in WAIT_MONITOR because geometric mean fusion collapses (P(struct)=0.00 ⇒ P(fused)=0.06 < 0.70), imposing a +Rs 2,14,000 cost penalty. Under KL-routing, the extreme evidence divergence (sym_KL=9.84 > 0.50) safely diverts 100% into human REVIEW.

Headline behavioral recall (95.45%) and F1 (0.917) are single-seed (Seed 42) results. Multi-seed evaluation reveals that behavioral recall varies dramatically from 27.03% to 95.45% (mean: 58.63%), whereas structural metrics remain stable (Precision: 78.3% - 100.0%).
